## 🏆 Hackathons

### ♻️ [BladiNadif — Predictive Dynamic Routing for Waste Collection](https://github.com/AouannarDoua)
> Ramadan AI Hackathon — Ministry of Digital Transition & Jazar Institut

Waste management system based on predictive dynamic routing, built for SOS Déchets (Oujda) with Sanae Ouadeh, Oualid Boutaleb and Imad Eddine Amraqui.

- 🛰️ IoT bin sensors + truck RFID tags + data warehouse as data sources
- 🌲 **Random Forest Classifier** predicting bin fill levels (**96.9% accuracy**)
- 🗺️ Automatic generation of optimized routes instead of fixed, random collection rounds
- 📊 "Smart Waste Governance" dashboard: predictive map, alerts, recommended tours
- **Stack:** `Python` `Random Forest` `IoT` `RFID` `Data Warehouse` `Google AI Studio`
- ⚡ **-66 stops/day** (-36.8%) | **-22% diesel consumption** | **-49,500 MAD/year per truck** | **-3.4 tons of CO2/year per truck**

### 🧠 [Strategeo — Geopolitical Financial Intelligence](https://github.com/Seddzz/strategeo)
> CDG Capital MIATHON 2025 — 72-hour responsible AI hackathon, Université Mohammed Premier, Oujda

Multi-agent financial intelligence platform developed by a team of 5 to address two financial intelligence challenges. The platform combines AI agents, real-time information retrieval, financial analysis, interactive visualization and automated reporting — helping Moroccan companies and investors anticipate how geopolitical risk hits their portfolio.

- 🌐 Real-time monitoring of geopolitical events, currencies and commodities (NewsAPI, ExchangeRate-API)
- 🤖 AI analysis of the company's own context via uploaded strategic documents
- 🚨 Personalized alert feed with an impact chain quantified in MAD and a recommended action
- 🌍 Interactive geopolitical risk globe + evolving Strategic Risk Score (30/60/90 days)
- 🔐 Responsible AI: no automated financial decisions, mandatory human approval, full logging
- **Stack:** `LangGraph` `Groq Llama 3.3 70B` `Tavily` `FastAPI` `SSE` `React` `D3.js` `ReportLab` `Firebase`
